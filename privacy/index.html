Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this is

Cartofia is a personal homelab project. The site, the arcade, the Minecraft server, the forum, and the account system all run on a single server owned and operated by one person. This policy describes what information is collected, why, and how it is handled.

Information we collect

  • Account data from your identity provider. When you sign in — directly with a Cartofia password, or through Google — the identity provider gives us your email, display name, profile picture URL, and a stable account identifier.
  • Profile content you create. Avatar, banner, bio, chosen theme, forum posts, achievements earned, and anything else you put on your public profile.
  • Game activity. Which browser games you played, roughly how long, win / loss outcomes, and achievements unlocked. Used to populate your public profile and stats.
  • Minecraft server activity. If you create a personal Minecraft server, we record its slug, assigned port, and timestamps for creation / last-activity. Minecraft worlds are stored on the host.
  • Linked third-party IDs. If you choose to link your Discord account, we store your Discord user ID so the bot can send you a direct message when your server is up.
  • Server logs. Standard web-server logs (IP address, URL, user-agent, timestamp) retained for a short rotation. Used for debugging and spam prevention.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To render your public profile and let other users find you.
  • To route Minecraft traffic to the right backend server.
  • To send a Discord DM when your personal server comes online, if you linked Discord.
  • To keep the service running and detect abuse.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or trackers.
  • We do not share your email with other users.
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models.

Third parties

  • Authentik — self-hosted identity provider on the same infrastructure. Handles passwords, OIDC flows, and group membership.
  • Google — only if you sign in with Google. We receive the standard OIDC claims (email, name, profile picture) and nothing else.
  • Cloudflare — DNS and CDN for cartofia.com. Can see request metadata (IP, path) but not account content.
  • Discord — only if you link your Discord account. The bot sends you a direct message when your personal Minecraft server starts.
  • playit.gg — forwards Minecraft TCP traffic from the public internet into the homelab. Can see connection metadata (IP, bytes).

Deletion

You can delete your personal Minecraft server at any time from the Minecraft page. User accounts and all associated profile content can be deleted on request — email manolachestefan13@gmail.com from the address attached to your Cartofia account and the account plus all content will be removed within 14 days.

Personal Minecraft servers that have had no activity for 90 days are automatically removed by the nightly cleanup job; the world is archived for an additional 90 days then deleted.

Security

Cartofia runs on a single homelab server. Reasonable care is taken — HTTPS everywhere, OIDC-authenticated APIs, secrets kept out of source control — but this is a personal project, not an enterprise. If a serious incident happens, affected users will be contacted by email.
